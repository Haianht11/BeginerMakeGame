Idle (4), Run (4), Attack (4), Hurt (2), Death (2).

per unit. Idle and Run loop. Attack, Hurt and Death are one-shots.

Sheet validation
16 distinct, occupied cells. Final sheet has zero cell crossings and zero pixels
touching the outside boundary. The smallest ground margin is 12 px. The runtime
spill check used a 10% safe margin to preserve the player's frame and ground
coordinates. The raw attack blade crosses a grid line. Component ownership
keeps the whole blade with the right pose before the runtime repack.
Unity 6000.6.8f1 imported all 16 slices and verified their rectangles, 32 PPU,
point filtering, shared feet pivot, sprite references, durations and loop settings.
