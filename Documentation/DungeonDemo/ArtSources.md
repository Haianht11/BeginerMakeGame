## Background prompt

Create a production-ready opaque raster background for a small 2D side-scrolling Unity platformer training room, very wide horizontal 16:9 game view, original dark fantasy subterranean dungeon. Straight orthographic side elevation, no isometric perspective. Native crisp high-bit pixel art style, deliberate pixel clusters, slate gray, steel blue, midnight indigo, desaturated cool colors, faint cold mist in the far architecture. Cinematic but readable at a glance. Make one contiguous room with masonry pillars and two receding Gothic stone arches. Iron chains visibly hanging in varied lengths from the upper wall and ceiling; a clearly legible large black wrought-iron prisoner cage hanging in the middle-left rear, and a second smaller barred cage in the far-right background. Distribute small clusters of lit candles along the walking route used by the player, with warm tiny amber flames and warm local halos against cold stone. Leave the central-right training area visually quiet against a dark rear arch to silhouette a target dummy that will be added separately. IMPORTANT TECHNICAL ART DIRECTION: a SINGLE continuous walkable stone platform running the entire width of the image all the way to both edges, with its hard flat straight top exactly 78 percent from the top. This is the only platform. Keep the top face very narrow, side profile, straight unbroken horizontal walking baseline. Below it fill the remaining bottom section with the thick dark masonry front face in shadow, no gaps. Candles may just touch this top line but nothing blocks movement above it. Left and right screen edges use recessed dark masonry pillars, visually enclosing the room. Give the center and lower area enough cool local illumination to read textures and movement. No letters, no text, no UI, no captions, no people, no target dummy, no weapon, no castle exterior, no frames or borders. Full bleed game background ready to import.

## Training dummy prompt

One transparent-background isolated raster game sprite of an inanimate medieval dungeon training dummy, total silhouette visible, dark fantasy 2D side-scrolling high-bit pixel art, crisp controlled chunky pixel clusters and very readable at small game scale. Slight three-quarter front view that works from either direction. An old wooden crossbar and post on a very short dark oak footed base, mounted straw sack humanoid torso and burlap wrapped head, straw peeking through, stubby horizontal padded arms, a clearly readable muted crimson and weathered bronze circular target fixed on the chest, iron bindings and cloth wraps. Worn gray teal and brown palette with a strong desaturated blue-gray rim light on upper left silhouette. Subtle rough material highlights, mostly dark cool design while the red target stands out. Straight waist and small solid wooden base at the lowest visible edge, isolated entire figure centered. Moderately tall narrow proportions, roughly 1.7 times as tall as wide. No human, no weapon, no blood, no environment, no floor, no cast shadow, no halo, no exterior border, no text. Transparent background.
